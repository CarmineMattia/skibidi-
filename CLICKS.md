# CLICKS - Customer Order Flow Analysis

## Current Click Path to Payment

### Guest Order Flow (Happy Path)
1. **Open App** → 1 click (land on menu)
2. **Add item to cart** → 1 click (press + button on product)
3. **Open cart** → 1 click (press cart button or bottom bar)
4. **Checkout** → 1 click (press "Procedi al Pagamento" button)
5. **Enter all details** → 1 click (on one-screen checkout)

Total clicks: 5
